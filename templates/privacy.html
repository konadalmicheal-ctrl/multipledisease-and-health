PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your information matters.

This is an educational student prototype. Read these limitations before entering information.

What this demo stores

Account name and email, a password hash, checklist summaries, and report file metadata are stored in the configured database. Uploaded files are stored in the configured upload directory.

Use sample data only

Do not upload real patient reports or sensitive medical information to a public demonstration deployment. The project has not undergone a healthcare privacy or security compliance review.

External Google Lens

Opening Google Lens takes you to an external service. This application does not automatically send your uploaded files to Google Lens. Review the external service's own terms before sharing any image.

Educational use

Checklist summaries are rule-based examples, not diagnoses or clinically validated predictions. They must not be used to make treatment decisions.
